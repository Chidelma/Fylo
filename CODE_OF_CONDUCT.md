# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

FYLO contributors, maintainers, users, and reviewers are expected to make
participation respectful, safe, and productive. Be considerate, assume good
faith while requiring evidence, welcome constructive disagreement, and focus
criticism on behavior, designs, and code rather than people.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, doxxing, or publication of another person's private information
without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews,
community spaces, release activities, and private communication made in an
official project capacity.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Security
vulnerabilities use the separate route in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers will
protect reporter confidentiality as far as reasonably possible, investigate
consistently, and may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily
restrict participation, or permanently remove a participant.

Maintainers who are the subject of a report must recuse themselves. Retaliation
against a reporter or witness is itself a violation.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant and is intentionally
maintained in concise project-specific language.
